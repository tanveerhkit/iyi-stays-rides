# IYI Rides — Product Presentation

## Cover
IYI Rides
The room is booked. The way there is too.
A mobility layer for every stay

## Slide 1
### The stay does not start at check-in
Guests still arrange the journey around their room booking separately.

- Airport pickup, station transfer, and local rides live outside the hotel journey.
- Switching between apps creates uncertainty around pickup points, fares, and support.
- IYI can connect accommodation with the moments before, during, and after the stay.

## Slide 2
### One journey. Fewer handoffs.
IYI Rides turns the hotel reservation into a useful mobility starting point.

- Prefill the hotel address, stay dates, guest contact, and arrival context.
- Let guests discover, book, track, and manage rides in the same trip view.
- Keep hotel and ride charges separate while making the relationship between them obvious.

## Slide 3
### Start with the moments that matter most
The MVP focuses on high-intent, high-context journeys.

| Journey | Guest promise |
|---|---|
| Airport pickup | Land, locate, and get to the front door with less uncertainty. |
| Station transfer | Move from platform to property without another planning task. |
| Local ride | Reach the next stop without losing the thread of the stay. |
| Return transfer | Plan the way back before the final day gets busy. |

## Slide 4
### The booking flow feels like a concierge handoff
A simple five-step path keeps the guest oriented.

1. **Discover:** See a ride prompt after booking or from the dedicated Rides entry point.
2. **Plan:** Choose pickup, destination, date, time, passengers, and luggage.
3. **Compare:** Review provider, vehicle, capacity, fare, inclusions, and cancellation terms.
4. **Confirm:** Pay and receive a ride confirmation linked to the hotel booking.
5. **Arrive:** Follow driver details, pickup guidance, status updates, and support.

## Slide 5
### Trust is designed into every decision
The product wins when it removes uncertainty before it adds convenience.

- Show the fare before confirmation and label estimates clearly.
- Surface vehicle capacity, provider identity, pickup instructions, and cancellation rules.
- Keep ride-specific support close for delays, no-shows, wrong vehicles, fare disputes, and safety issues.
- Treat partner licensing, screening, insurance, and incident response as launch requirements.

## Slide 6
### A partner layer that can scale city by city
IYI owns the customer experience while approved mobility partners fulfill rides.

- An orchestration layer normalizes quote, booking, status, cancellation, refund, and receipt flows.
- City-level configuration controls zones, operating hours, vehicle types, and provider coverage.
- Idempotent booking and reconciliation prevent duplicate reservations and financial leakage.
- Operations can inspect partner responses, intervene in incidents, and manage approved refunds.

## Slide 7
### Success starts with fulfillment, not assortment
The first release should be measured on reliability and confidence.

| Signal | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| Booking success | Can a guest complete the journey without partner or payment failure? |
| Fulfillment rate | Do confirmed rides actually happen? |
| On-time pickup | Is the core promise dependable in each city? |
| Support contact rate | Where does the experience still create uncertainty? |
| Ride contribution margin | Can the service scale into a sustainable business? |

## Slide 8
### Pilot narrow. Learn quickly. Expand with proof.
A focused rollout protects the brand while building the operating model.

- **Discovery:** Select partners, validate legal responsibilities, and choose high-coverage pilot cities.
- **Internal pilot:** Test scheduled transfers, payments, cancellations, support, and reconciliation.
- **Limited beta:** Expose a small share of eligible guests and monitor fulfillment and satisfaction.
- **Expansion:** Add cities, local rides, provider redundancy, and stronger contextual merchandising.
- **Next horizon:** Evaluate two-wheelers, rentals, hourly rides, and intercity mobility separately.

## Slide 9
### IYI Rides makes the stay feel complete
The product idea is simple: one less thing to figure out around every room booking.

- Make arrival and departure easier to plan.
- Build confidence with clear details and responsive support.
- Create a scalable mobility layer without taking on uncontrolled fleet risk.

**Next decision:** Approve the scheduled-transfer pilot and begin partner validation.
